# P5: Keyboard, cards and order

## What was built

**Keyboard in the open Ultra panel.** `keys::map` turns a key into an action; `ui.rs` applies it.

| Key | Action | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| Arrows | Lock the first card, or move the lock one step on the grid | Ultra |
| Enter | Open the locked card on CNBC | Ultra, a lock |
| C | Open or close the chart | Ultra |
| S | Open or close the symbol picker | Ultra |
| Ctrl+C | Copy the locked card's line, or every price with no lock | Both builds (Classic copies every price) |
| Delete | Remove the locked card from the strip | Ultra, a lock |
| Ctrl+Left, Ctrl+Right | Move the locked card one place in the order | Ultra, a lock |

Esc and the paging keys keep their existing handling.

**Card menu.** Each card's menu gets Move left, Move right, Move first and Remove from strip.

**Strip clicks.** On a docked or cornered Ultra strip, a click on a symbol locks that card and opens the panel on it. A click between symbols, the floating strip and Classic work as before.

**Order.** Moving a card changes the Symbols catalogue, so the order survives later ticking and unticking of symbols. Hidden symbols keep their place in the catalogue.

## Decisions

- Removing the locked card passes the lock to the card that takes its place (the next one, or the previous one for the last card).
- Each symbol on the strip has a click rectangle recorded while the text is laid out. A click between rectangles does nothing new.
- The Ultra footer names the keys only when the line fits beside the left-hand text. Otherwise it keeps the old hint.
- A key that `map` does not answer, and any key on the strip or during a morph, goes to `DefWindowProcW`.
- `config::move_row` and `move_first` work on a copy and commit only on success. A false return changes nothing, the catalogue included.
- A hand-added row that is moved is adopted into the catalogue. A move that fails does not adopt it.
- Ctrl+Left and Ctrl+Right do nothing without a locked card. Ctrl+Up and Ctrl+Down do nothing.

## Tests

The keyboard map, card stepping and the order functions have unit tests in `src/keys.rs` and `src/config.rs`. The full suite passes except `ui::tests::a_theme_switch_takes_the_new_themes_face`, which fails under Wine and is not part of this package.

## Unverified

These were not exercised on Windows:

- Real key presses and keyboard focus, including whether the panel keeps the keyboard after a click on the strip or the hotkey.
- Real strip clicks on each placement.
- The card menu entries and their effect on the strip.
