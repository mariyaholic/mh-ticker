# W2 notes: Alerts that say when they cannot see (writer)

Test totals are Wine runs; the one known failure (`ui::tests::a_theme_switch_takes_the_new_themes_face`) is not mine.
New pure modules, all in `scripts/check-state-linux.sh`: `governor.rs`, `watch.rs`, `retry.rs`.

## H6 and C10: the poll governor

- `governor::interval(base, quiet_for, locked, armed, settings)`: quiet 10 min gives 15 s, quiet 30 min gives 60 s,
  locked gives 300 s, and armed holds the answer to 15 s (or the user's own base when that is slower).
  Both ticks off returns the base for every input. `governor::stale_after(stale_after_s, governed_s)` is
  twice the governed interval when that is larger than the configured limit, and the configured limit when
  the governor is off (`governed_s` 0).
- Settings: `slow_when_quiet` and `rest_when_locked` are `Option<bool>` in `Config`. Unset means Ultra on,
  Classic off (`Config::governor(ultra)`). Two menu ticks in the Poll every submenu. A saved choice wins.
- Wiring: `Board` carries `gov`, `locked`, `armed` (UI writes) and `governed_s`, `slowed` (feed writes). `Feed::run`
  asks `governed()` after each poll; `governor::Quiet` is a local there and counts monotonic seconds, so a
  wall-clock jump cannot fake a quiet half hour. Any `kick` restarts the quiet clock. The UI wakes the feed
  (`wake_feed`) on expand, the hotkey and an unlock, and `sync_gov` (called from `save_cfg` and `on_feed`)
  kicks when a settings or alert change could shorten a slowed wait. Wakes do nothing while both ticks are
  off, so Classic's polling is untouched.
- Lock and unlock: `WM_WTSSESSION_CHANGE`, registered by calling `WTSRegisterSessionNotification` directly
  (windows-sys feature `Win32_System_RemoteDesktop`, enabled in `Cargo.toml`; `Cargo.lock` unchanged). The
  window unregisters on `WM_DESTROY`. The app no longer loads `wtsapi32` by name. `session_changed` writes
  `Board::locked` and wakes on unlock, outside `with_ui` for the kick.
- Decisions: the feed's failure back-off (up to 60 s) is kept as it was, so a failing network can be polled
  slower than 15 s while an alert is armed; the governor itself never exceeds the cap. The chart thread's
  hour-of-bars refresh (every 10 min) is not governed. `alerts::reference_price` judges freshness with the same
  governed limit as the fire path (`governor::stale_after`), so a preset and the fire path agree on what is fresh.
  Pinned by `a_preset_and_the_fire_path_agree_on_what_is_fresh`, seen failing first.
- Effect, from a scripted clock (`a_quiet_two_hours_costs_far_fewer_polls`): a market that moves for 5 min and
  is then quiet for the rest of 2 h, base 3 s. Polls: base 2,400; governed 465; governed with an alert armed
  720. Bytes were not measured (no live feed, and the meter counts real responses).
- Tests seen failing first (mutations): the armed cap removed (3 governor tests fail); `publish` ignoring
  `governed_s` (`publish_uses_the_governed_stale_limit` fails).

## K1 each crossing, K2 fast move

- `Alert::crossing` and `Config::alert_crossing`; `Alert::latched` (not saved). The Repeat submenu is one
  radio: every cooldown (default), fire once, each crossing; the choice rewrites every alert and clears latches.
  Once wins if both are set in a hand-edited file. Band: 0.1 % of the level (0.1 point for a day move,
  half a fast threshold). A crossing alert ignores the cooldown; the band is its rate limit. Stale and
  fallback quotes are skipped before anything is judged, so they neither fire nor re-arm.
- `alerts::check_with(cfg, board, now, series)`; `check` is the test shorthand with no history.
  `alerts::fast_move(samples, now, price, window, max_gap)` needs a sample at or before the window start and
  no gap over three polls (the governed interval, or `poll_s`) up to now. `Alert::fast` is an index plus one into
  `FAST_MOVES` (1 % in 5 min, 2 % in 15, 3 % in 60). The exact threshold fires (1e-9 slack for float error).
  The samples are `panel::History` (`series_of`), which includes the hour of 1-minute bar closes; those are
  60 s apart, so at a 3 s poll they count as a gap and a fast move waits for live samples.
- One test per mode for stale and fallback: `in_every_repeat_mode_a_stale_quote_fires_nothing`
  (cooldown, once, crossing, fast; it also proves each alert fires on a fresh quote first). Mutation: the stale
  test loosened, it fails on "every cooldown: old quote".

## H7 gaps

- `watch::Watch::step(now, fresh, armed, threshold)`; threshold is the larger of 5 min and ten polls
  (`watch::threshold`). Events `Blind` (once, when the span passes the threshold: tooltip line) and
  `Back { from, to, jump }` (one Recent line, `02:10 to 02:31 not checked (no fresh price)`; a clock jump says
  `no checks ran, the PC slept`). `alerts::checkable` gives armed (a level on a shown symbol) and fresh
  (at least one such symbol has a fresh CNBC quote). Decision: one fresh symbol is enough, because a closed
  market for one symbol would otherwise make a line every night.
- Optional webhook notice (`Config::gap_notice`, off, a tick in the Webhook submenu, grey with no webhook):
  one POST when checking is back, text `Alerts could not be checked from HH:MM to HH:MM.` Not sent while
  muted or in quiet hours. No price anywhere (test checks the digits). `WM_ALERT` bit 3 marks it; it has no log line.

## H8 digest

- `watch::Hush` is stepped by the feed tick with `alerts::silence`; the tick that finds the silence over asks
  `alerts::digest(log, since)` and shows one balloon (`3 alerts while muted. See Alerts > Recent.`), and the same
  text goes to the panel footer note. Zero entries: nothing. No timer was added (review: `grep SetTimer` shows none new).

## A13 retry

- `retry::next(failure, attempt)`, `retry::run(send, sleep)`: a network error or a 5xx waits 3 s and tries once
  more; a 4xx, anything else and a second failure never. Outcome `sent on retry` (`Outcome::SentOnRetry`,
  `WM_ALERT` bit 2, footer note `webhook sent on retry`). `alerts::spawn_post` counts `IN_FLIGHT`;
  `alerts::wait_for_posts` (2 s cap, a waiting retry goes at once) runs in `WM_DESTROY` after the tray icon and
  the save. One extra POST only after a failure; nothing is queued on disk.

## Review

Two fixes after the review round:

- Lock and unlock now call `WTSRegisterSessionNotification` directly (see the governor section), not `wtsapi32` by name.
- `reference_price` uses the governed freshness limit, as the fire path does (see the governor section).

Decisions the checker accepted:

- The failure back-off can exceed 15 s while an alert is armed. Every quote is then stale, nothing can fire, and
  the gap line reports it.
- A gap needs every armed alert on a shown symbol to be blind. One fresh symbol is enough to show no gap.
- No gap notice is sent while muted.
- The two governor settings, unset, mean Ultra on and Classic off.
