# W4 Reach: writer's note

## What was built

- **M5** `src/contrast.rs` (pure): `luminance`, `ratio`, the thresholds (text 4.5, direction 3.0) and the system colour table with two fixed palettes (`HC_BLACK`, `HC_WHITE`). Test `theme::every_theme_reads_text_and_direction_against_its_background` judges price and label (4.5) and up and down (3.0) against the card and every colour a theme's cycle passes through (stops and the 48-step mixes). Seen failing first on five pairs (below).
- **M4** `src/updown.rs` (pure) and `theme::with_updown` (a static table, same pointer per mode). `Config::updown` (`red_green`, `blue_orange`, unset is today) and `hollow_up`. Hollow up candles are outlines. Menu: Accessibility.
- **M3** `Theme::from_system`, `theme::system` (one leaked theme per distinct palette), `ui::read_contrast` (`SPI_GETHIGHCONTRAST`, `GetSysColor`; no Cargo change needed), `WM_SYSCOLORCHANGE` and `WM_SETTINGCHANGE`. `Config::contrast`: unset is on for Ultra, off for Classic. The theme draws on the window colour only; foregrounds are window text, hotlight (rise) and gray text (dim); the accent is window text. Fills become outlines (`panel::plate`), cards get a frame, the locked card a thicker one, up candles are hollow, and the sign carries direction in text.
- **M6** `src/motion.rs`: `frames(follow, system)`. `Config::animate`: unset follows Windows on Ultra, always animates on Classic. One step is a zero-length morph (one resize, one repaint).
- **M2** `src/typed.rs`: span, time, `mute_until`, cooldown parsers and four fixed refusal sentences. No new config fields. Prompts: quiet hours (type hours), mute until a time, hotkey (uses `hotkey::parse` unchanged, stored canonical), cooldown per symbol (in each symbol's alert submenu, applies to that symbol's alert).
- **M1** `keys::map` grows (R, Z, A, F10, F1, menu key), `map_shifted` for Shift+Left/Right, `step_candle`, `HELP` table (F1 text and the "every action has a line" test). `Layout::pointer_for_bar`, `Layout::cross`. A keyed crosshair is snapped to the candle's close by the painter (`Scene::aim_snap`); a pointer move takes it back and repaints the whole window. P5's tests are unchanged (`map` keeps its signature; `is_bound` is untouched; new keys are routed by `is_reach_key`).
- **G3** `src/zoom.rs`; `ui::pdpi` = DPI times zoom, used for every panel geometry, font, hit test and the scene; the strip keeps the real DPI. A zoom change drops the saved and remembered frames and invalidates the whole window.
- **M7** `src/spoken.rs`; `sync_title` once per feed tick and when the lock moves. Classic's title is never touched.

New pure modules (in `scripts/check-state-linux.sh`): `contrast`, `updown`, `zoom`, `motion`, `typed`, `spoken`. `keys` and `chartview` grew.

## Decisions

- Text means price and label. `dim` (stale and secondary text) is measured and printed but not gated: it is 1.6 to 4.2 across the catalogue, and gating it would change nearly every Ultra still. Open item for the checker.
- Backgrounds judged: the card and every mix of the three stops. Up and down are also judged against the card.
- Red/green swaps each theme's own two colours; blue/orange is one pair for dark themes (130,185,255 and 255,160,60) and one for light themes (20,90,200 and 176,84,0); every theme and mode is tested at 3 to 1.
- The window name is `Gold 4,012.30, up 0.4 percent - MH Ticker Ultra` (lock) or `MH Ticker Ultra, 6 symbols, 4 up, 2 down`.
- Classic's strip and panel keep `u.dpi` and MIDNIGHT; Classic gets only the contrast and animation ticks and the typed hotkey.

## Tests seen failing first

- the contrast test (five pairs below the floor, listed above);
- the help-line test caught `LockFirst` with no line;
- `a_keyed_crosshair...` first failed on candles that do not fit the plot (the painter drops them; the test now skips those widths);
- the blue/orange check first failed on gameboy (3.00, under the floor); lightening the dark blue fixed it.

New-behaviour tests (parsers, `frames`, `spoken_line`, zoom, hollow, the red_green pixel swap on a retro theme without antialiasing) were written with the code and pass.

## Unverified

Real key presses and focus (Shift+arrows, F10, F1, the menu key via `WM_CONTEXTMENU`), the real system colours and the change message, the Windows animation setting and the one-step morph on a real display, screen readers and Alt+Tab reading the window name, and the zoom path through `ui` (tested through `zoom::effective_dpi` and the painter at that DPI, not through the window). A pointer move after a keyed crosshair is assumed to arrive as `WM_MOUSEMOVE`.

## Review

- The checker looked at the four tuned Ultra stills (glass, ega, gameboy, c64) before and after the tuning and
  accepted them. They are now in the checker's stills baseline.
- The committed copies of those four in `shots/` were not regenerated. The committed theme stills already differed
  from the Wine-rendered baseline before W4, so the committed files still show the colours from before tuning.
- The `dim` colour (secondary and stale text, 1.6 to 4.2 to 1 across the catalogue) is not gated. Gating it would
  change nearly every Ultra still. This is left open for the owner. The contrast theme is the answer for users who
  need more contrast than the Ultra themes give.
- The checker added the `[[example]]` entry for `w4` (`examples/w4.rs`, `required-features = ["shots"]`) to `Cargo.toml`.
